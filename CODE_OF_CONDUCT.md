# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be considerate, accept
constructive feedback, focus discussion on the work, and respect privacy and differing
experiences. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, publication of private
information, and other unprofessional conduct are unacceptable.

Project maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these expectations.
Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. They will review reports fairly,
protect confidentiality where possible, and avoid conflicts of interest.
